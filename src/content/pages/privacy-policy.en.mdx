## Introduction

TattooGenerator.online ("Tattoo Generator", "we", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to handling your personal data transparently. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our AI tattoo generator, virtual try-on simulator, font generator, and related tools, how we use it, and how you can control or delete it.

## Information We Collect

1. **Account Information** — When you sign in with email or Google OAuth, we store your email address, display name, and authentication identifiers needed to manage your account, saved designs, and credit balance.
2. **Uploaded Body Photos & Tattoo Artwork (Virtual Try-On)** — Photos you upload in the 2D Virtual Tattoo Try-On workspace are processed locally in your browser session for previewing placement and are never published to public galleries or shared with other users. If you explicitly save a design to your private account library, it is stored solely for your account access.
3. **AI Prompts & Generated Designs** — Prompts, selected styles, and generated tattoo previews are processed to fulfill your generation requests and saved to your private account history while signed in.
4. **Payment Information** — One-time credit pack purchases are processed by third-party payment providers (such as Stripe). We receive transaction confirmations and order records, and we never store full credit card numbers on our servers.
5. **Essential Cookies & Diagnostics** — We use essential cookies for sign-in sessions and language preferences (English or Chinese), plus aggregated diagnostic logs to keep the service reliable.

## How We Use Your Information

- Deliver tattoo design generation, stencil exports, font previews, and virtual try-on features.
- Process credit pack purchases, deliver credits, and send order or support communications.
- Prevent abuse, automated scraping, and unauthorized account access.
- Respond to refund, privacy, and technical support requests.

## Data Sharing & Third-Party Processing

We do **not** sell your personal information or uploaded photos. We share data only with vetted infrastructure and AI/payment providers strictly necessary to operate TattooGenerator.online (hosting, database, payment processing, and image generation APIs), under confidentiality and data-protection obligations, or when required by law.

## Data Retention & Your Rights

You can view, export, or request deletion of your account data and saved designs at any time. To request full account and data deletion, email **support@tattoogenerator.online** from your account email address and we will process your request promptly.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our tools evolve. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your photos and data are handled, contact us directly at **support@tattoogenerator.online**.
